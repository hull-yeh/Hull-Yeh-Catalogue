ocean.wav (Started: 2026)

Bandcamp | Youtube

Just a little Plunderphonics project under the Vaporwave genre I've been working on. I first got into vaporwave 10 years ago and this internet genre introduced me to 80s Japanese City Pop.

I started doing vaporwave cause I also wanted to share to fellow-vaporwave listeners, classic OPM music the same way I was introduced to Japanese city pop. And hence why some of my vaprwave bops have old OPM samples. I've been producing and currently producing original compositions for my vaporwave works as well.

Aug 2026 update: actually, I don't just dabble around in classic OPM anymore. I've been leaning deeper to signalwave lately so recently, I've been experimenting with various old pieces of Philippine media in general. This project is still continuously evolving into new forms. There's definitely one thing that will clearly explain what this experimental project is in a nutshell: Plunderphonics.

Just a couple of years ago, I stumbled across frutiger aero and its nostalgia essence reminded me so much of the strange nostalgic feeling I felt when I first discovered vaporwave. So here I am, back to my vaporwave hyperfixation in the big 2026

---> In a way, this sound collage/plunderphonics project serves as a practice for me with sound remixing that could possibly help me re-shape and evolve my music style in my original compositions haha
