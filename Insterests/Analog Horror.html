ANALOG HORROR

You might be wondering why isn't this included in the films category. And that is because, it's not a movie genre. It's an internet subgenre. Though it did draw inspiration from found footage films like the Blair Witch Project, or footage horror like the Paranormal Activity

Analog horror is an internet sub-genre that started in the 2010s, as the name states, this genre emulates analog media (television broadcast, VHS and stuff), and common themes that come along with it are hijacking broadcast transmissions, uncanny valley, ominous messages, static glitches, etc. Check out this fandom wiki for more explanation cause I don't think my explanation do this justice lmao

Of all the horror genres that exist out there, Analog horror is definitely my top favorite. Why? This is the only horror genre that gives me genuine fear. Unlike the generic horror that feeds you into anticipation of jumpscares, this genre normally don't have any jumpscares, but it hits you psychologically (for me atleast). Psychological horror is 10x scarier for me than any other horror subgenres

I kid you not, even if I say that I love Analog Horror, it's sometimes hard for me for finish the whole series because of how terrifying the videos makes me feel haha. Like seriously, sometimes I had to watch it along with Matpat just to not feel alone watching it lmao. (I miss Matpat huhu)
