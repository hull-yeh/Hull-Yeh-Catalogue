Photographer: Who Knows???

You might be wondering why I like this photo. It's because, if you're into history of photography, you know that back in the early years of photography, the process of taking a picture was long exposure to light meaning back then, in order to take a photo, you have to stay still for a long time just to get a single photo. That explains why people in really old pictures looked serious and had no smiles in their faces. That's just one of the processes of taking a photo, developing it is a whole nother process. And I know I explained that badly so go search it up yourself lol.
ANYWAY, why the photo of this Chinese guy? It's because its origin was mysterious. No one knows who took the photo and when exactly was it taken. Because of the big smile pose he chose to make, in a way made it difficult to identify cause seeing a big smile in photos is such a modern thing! Imagine tho if his face hurt after sitting in that pose for so long just to make the photo possible. lol

I'm also a bit skeptical cause maybe it could've been staged and was actually taken in our modern times, but this image is literally in a museum ( American Museum of Natural History) and is claimed to be taken between 1901 and 1904 so, who knows???? This what made me like this photo. ~The mystery~




Pale Blue Dot

That tiny dot is the Earth. This photo is taken by Voyager 1, the farthest spacecraft from Earth. NASA Launched Voyager 1, as well as Voyager 2 in the 1970s, and as of now (2026), Voyager 1 is now outside of our solar system.
