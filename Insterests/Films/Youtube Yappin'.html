Cinema Therapy


Check out their YT Channel here

Jonathan Decker (licensed therapist ) and Alan Seawright (professional filmmaker), combines film and psychology as their content. They do film analysis, focusing on the psychology of film characters. They've got a handful of series such as "Psychology of a Hero", "Villain Theraphy", "Toxic or Not", "Relationship Advice" and many more.

They're prolly my top favorite youtube channel for film-related content. If you like films AND Psychology, I demand you to check them out. THIS IS A THREAT!

Swofford


Check out their YT Channel here

My Old Hollywood/Vintage loving-ass instantly fell in love with his channel the moment I discovered him. Not exactly doing old film analysis BUT Swofford makes content on film history and what goes on in film productions in the past, such as how actors makeup looked like back when films are still in Black & White, what happened to Hollywood when broadcast television came around and so many more interesting history. He also dabbles around vintage fashion. He also do music too so that's dope!
