Heneral Luna (2015)


This is a trilogy, Heneral Luna (2015), Goyo: Ang Batang Heneral (2018), and Quezon (2025). What I love about TBA's approach in these historical film as that they portrayed our historical figures more human than how we percieve them. What I mean by that is that unlike how we usually picture or portray our historical figures where we portray them as the remarkable hero that help shape our country and all that shebang, with these films, they actully dug up some dirt about them and portrayed their flaws in the film, which I think made them more human than a historical figure we glaze upon because of their contribution in our country. It's in a way to remind us that we humans are not black and white, that sometimes, a person who did remarkable things can also have their bad side, and vice versa. I'm yapping too much about this now, just go and watch this trilogy, it's great
