Poolsuite.net

Do you like vaporwave, future funk or retro-styled disco-pop? Or do you like to get a feel of the 90s pc interface? Or what if you like both? You might enjoy Poolsuite!

They used to be called "Poolside FM". Basically, it's kind of like a radio station but not exactly? Y'know, just go to the website and experience it yourself! haha It's a fun website, go check it out!
(Btw, according to wikipedia, it's an "internet radio")



Radio Garden

Do you still like listening to the radio in our day and age of modern listening on dems spotifys, apple music, youtube music and all that jazz?

You might want to check out Radio Garden. With this website, you can get to listen to different radio station all over the world and check out what's currently on in those locations, REAL TIME.
