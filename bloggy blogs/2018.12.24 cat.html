Cat in a Helmet

I was in Marcos Highway with mom but it's so quiet. Literally there's no cars or any vehicles passing by. The road is clean. 

As we were walking up the quiet high way, we saw a motorcycle helmet, in the middle of the road moving. And I was like, "wtf??", observing it, I saw a gray tail sticking up outside the helmet. I peeked and see a cat. Turns out, the cat was trying to cross the road with the helmet for protection. We just stood there and watch the cat cross the road. 

He successfully crossed the road and went out the helmet. After that, a fuck ton of vehicles arrived. It's like the scene from the movie "Walk of Shame" where the girl ran through the other side of the road and after successfully crossing, the carpocalypse began. 

And now there's traffic. We tried looking for the cat but we couldn't find it. And then a few minutes later, we saw it crossing back and fourth without the helmet this time. The cat almost got hit by cars several times already but the cat juke 'em. 

And then out of nowhere, along the traffic, the guy in a brown g-wagon opened his door car and a huge doggo jumped out of the car. The doggo chased the cat.  The cat was caught on the sidewalk. The doggo bit the cat and went back to the g. The cat was paralyzed. It can't move its body and it's crying in pain. 

Out of nowhere, a vet suddenly showed up and took the cat. For some reason, we went to the er with the vet. And they put the cat in a surgery, and we're watching. Actually a crowd of people is watching the surgery. Those people are just suddenly there. No idea where they came from. 

And then I woke up
